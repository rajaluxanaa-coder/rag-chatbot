ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (AI)
AI is the simulation of human intelligence in machines programmed to think and learn. AI can perform tasks like visual perception, speech recognition, decision-making, and translation.

MACHINE LEARNING (ML)
ML is a subset of AI that enables systems to learn from data without explicit programming. Algorithms build mathematical models to make predictions.

DEEP LEARNING (DL)
DL uses artificial neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns in data. It's inspired by the human brain structure.

RAG (RETRIEVAL-AUGMENTED GENERATION)
RAG combines information retrieval with generative AI. It retrieves relevant information from a knowledge base then generates accurate responses.

NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING (NLP)
NLP helps computers understand human language. It's used in chatbots, translation, and text analysis.

NEURAL NETWORKS
Neural networks are computing systems inspired by biological neural networks. They consist of connected nodes (neurons) that process information.

SUPERVISED LEARNING
A type of ML where models are trained on labeled data. The algorithm learns to map inputs to outputs.

UNSUPERVISED LEARNING
ML where models find patterns in unlabeled data. Used for clustering and dimensionality reduction.

REINFORCEMENT LEARNING
ML where agents learn to make decisions by receiving rewards or penalties. Used in robotics and game AI.

TRANSFORMERS
A neural network architecture that uses attention mechanisms. Used in modern AI like GPT and BERT.

CHATBOTS
AI programs that simulate human conversation. They use NLP to understand user queries and generate responses.

COMPUTER VISION
AI field that enables computers to interpret visual information from images and videos.

NARROW AI VS GENERAL AI
Narrow AI is designed for specific tasks (like Siri). General AI can perform any intellectual task humans can.

ETHICS IN AI
Important considerations include bias, privacy, transparency, and accountability in AI systems.

AI APPLICATIONS
Healthcare: Disease diagnosis and drug discovery
Finance: Fraud detection and trading
Transportation: Self-driving cars
Education: Personalized learning
Customer Service: Chatbots
